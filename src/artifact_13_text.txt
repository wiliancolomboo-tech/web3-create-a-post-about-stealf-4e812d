How does Stealf work?

1️⃣ Ephemeral ECDH: Sender combines recipient's View & Spend keys to generate an unlinkable, one-time PDA.
2️⃣ No Relayers: Zero mixer pools or compliance blacklists. 
3️⃣ Selective Audit: Recipient shares View Key with auditors while keeping Spend Keys cold.

Full open-source engine: https://github.com/stealf-solana/core
